The Gates You'll Actually Use

For about 90% of quantum programming, you'll repeatedly encounter a relatively small set.

Single-Qubit Gates
Gate	Purpose
I	Identity (does nothing)
X	Flip 0 ↔ 1
Y	Flip + phase
Z	Phase flip
H	Hadamard
S	Quarter phase rotation
T	Eighth phase rotation
SX	Square-root of X
RX	Rotation around X-axis
RY	Rotation around Y-axis
RZ	Rotation around Z-axis


----

Two-Qubit Gates

These are where quantum computing becomes interesting.

Examples include:

CX (Controlled-X / CNOT)
CY
CZ
CH
SWAP
iSWAP
ECR

Most quantum algorithms rely heavily on these because they allow qubits to interact.

---------

Three-Qubit Gates

Examples:

Toffoli (CCX)
Fredkin (CSWAP)

These are less common but extremely important in reversible computing and error correction.
